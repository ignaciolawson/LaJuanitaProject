# Branding & Visual Identity Guidelines

## Overview & Brand Vibe
- **Brand Aesthetic:** Dark, premium, modern electronic music studio / tech brand.
- **Tone & Mood:** High-end, professional, immersive, night-mode aesthetic with high contrast accents. Focuses on music production, DJ culture, and technical equipment excellence.
- **Visual Direction:** Sleek dark backgrounds with bold typography and sharp crimson red accent highlights. Clean and scalable minimal electronic tech branding.

---

## Color Palette

### Primary Colors
- **Background Deep Neutral:** `#0B0B0B` (Dark dark gray / near black)
- **Card / Surface Dark:** `#121212` (Elevated dark container background)
- **Card Border Subdued:** `#1F1F1F` / `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` (Subtle outline for dark depth)

### Accent Colors
- **Brand Primary Red:** `#E52328` (Vivid Crimson Red — CTAs, highlight text, icons, buttons)
- **Accent Red Hover/Glow:** `#FF2A30` (Brighter red for interactive states)
- **Subtle Red Tint:** `rgba(229, 35, 40, 0.15)` (Background fills for badges / tags)

### Typography & Neutrals
- **Text Primary (Headings/Key Text):** `#FFFFFF` (Pure White)
- **Text Secondary (Body/Descriptions):** `#A1A1AA` (Muted Light Gray)
- **Text Subdued / Placeholders:** `#666666` (Mid Gray)

---

## Typography & Text Styling

- **Primary Heading Font:** Modern Geometric Sans-Serif (e.g., *Inter*, *Plus Jakarta Sans*, *Syne*, or *Outfit*)
  - **Weights:** Heavy / Bold (700-900) for H1 and section titles.
  - **Styling:** Bold contrast using accent red inline for emphasis (e.g., `La Juanita <span style="color:#E52328">Studio</span>`).
- **Body & Subtitle Font:** Clean Sans-Serif (e.g., *Inter*, *DM Sans*)
  - **Weights:** Regular (400) to Medium (500).
  - **Line Height:** Relaxed (`1.5` to `1.6`) for clear readability on dark backgrounds.

---

## Brand Tone of Voice & Copywriting Style

- **Target Audience:** DJs, music producers, electronic music artists, event organizers, and sound enthusiasts.
- **Language & Tone:** 
  - Direct, motivating, professional, and inspiring.
  - Clear value propositions focusing on equipment quality, sound performance, and professional career growth.
  - Direct call-to-actions (e.g., *"Llevá tu sonido al siguiente nivel"*, *"Reservar Ahora"*).

---

## Core Visual Elements & Style Rules

1. **Dark Mode First:** Pure dark base to evoke studio, club, and electronic music culture.
2. **Red Highlight Accents:** Red is used selectively to draw immediate attention to key conversion points (primary action buttons, badge borders, active service highlights).
3. **Contrast & Hierarchy:** Stark contrast between pure white bold titles and desaturated gray body copy.
4. **Imagery & Atmosphere:** Darkened, warm, moody photography showing professional DJ gear (Pioneer CDJ/DJM), studio lighting, and session atmosphere.
